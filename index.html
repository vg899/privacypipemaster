Welcome to Pipe Master! This Privacy Policy is created by Harshvardhan Tiwari. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what information we do and do not collect when you use our mobile game.

💡 Short Summary: We don't collect your name, email, photo or location. We only use Google AdMob ads which may use anonymous Advertising ID to show relevant ads.

1 Information We DO NOT Collect

Pipe Master does NOT collect any user personal data such as:
  • Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number
  • Profile Picture or Personal Images/Videos
  • Precise Location (GPS)
  • Contacts or Call Logs

All your game progress, levels and scores are stored locally on your device only.

2 Information Collected by Third Parties

We allow trusted third-party companies to serve ads in our game. These are mainly Google AdMob & Google Play Services.

They may collect anonymous information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (to show relevant ads)
  • Device type, Android version, device model
  • General location (city/country level), IP address
  • App interaction and browsing activity for ad performance

You can opt-out or reset your Advertising ID anytime: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID

3 How We Use Anonymous Data

  • To display ads and keep the game 100% free for you
  • To improve game performance, fix bugs and add new levels
  • To understand how users play, so we can make it more fun

4 Permissions We Use

Pipe Master requests only INTERNET permission to load ads. We do NOT request Camera, Microphone, Contacts or Location permissions.

5 Children's Privacy

Our game is rated for Everyone and is safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately.

6 Data Security & Sharing

Since we don't collect personal data, we don't share it with anyone. Anonymous ad data is handled securely by Google under their own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy

7 Your Rights

You have full control. You can uninstall the game anytime to delete all local data. You can also disable personalized ads from your device settings.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the Effective Date.
